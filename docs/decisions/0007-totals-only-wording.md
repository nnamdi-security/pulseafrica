# 0007. "Totals Only" replaces the "Anonymous" badge and ticker

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-19)
- **Deciders:** Nnamdi (project owner)
- **Related:** [PRIV-02](../AUDIT.md#priv-02), [decision 0003](0003-privacy-and-methodology-copy.md), roadmap decision D9, Milestone 2

## Context

Decision 0003 corrected the privacy sentences, but every page still carried a footer badge "Anonymous Data" and a ticker item "Anonymous by design", and the home page had a heading "Anonymous and independent". Answers are stored with the respondent's account, so "anonymous" is not true.

## Decision

**D9:** on all 9 pages, the footer badge reads **"Totals Only"** and the ticker value reads **"Published as totals only"**. The home-page heading becomes **"Private and independent"**, and its paragraph keeps to the decision 0003 sentence.

## Alternatives considered

- **Remove the badge and the ticker item:** no claim at all, but a visible gap in the shared shell.

## Consequences

- No public page claims anonymity. The methodology page's mention of "anonymous response design" stays with METH-02 (Milestone 4).
- Any new copy describing privacy must stay consistent with 0003 and this record.
